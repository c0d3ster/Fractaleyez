- [ ] #47 Replace vs additive preset loading. Today picking a preset replaces the whole screen: a layer the preset leaves out is off. Add a second mode, additive, where picking a preset applies only the layers it includes and leaves every other layer as it is (its enabled state and its values). Needs your call before it can be planned: the name and where the control lives. The design below is settled.
  - Rules: the merge base for a layer the preset omits is a parameter of the load: replace uses the defaults (layer off), additive uses the live config. A layer is included when its sections are stored (`presentLayers` in `src/config/mergeLayers.ts`). The global sections (`user`, `audio`, `effects`, `color`) are always replaced in both modes. `layers.order` loads with `applyRelativeOrder(currentOrder, stored)`, so included layers rearrange among the slots they hold and omitted layers keep their place. Opacity and blend mode apply only for included layers. Additive never turns a layer off (absence means "leave it alone"); to remove a layer, toggle it or load in replace mode. The reset baseline is the merged result at load, so a section the preset never touched resets to what it was when loaded. `@user` references on a slot the preset does not touch stay as they are.
  - Already in place (`#227`, `#228`): enabled is derived from stored sections, `layers.order` is stored as the relative order of the included layers, `applyRelativeOrder` takes a base order, and `keepDisabledLayerSections` already keeps live values for an off layer a preset omits. The work is `retrieveConfigPreset` in `ConfigProvider.tsx` (pass the merge base and the mode) plus the control.
  - Decisions needed: (1) naming: "Replace" and "Stack", "Swap" and "Layer on", or a plain switch such as "Keep my other layers"; (2) where it lives (a toggle in the Presets header, a modifier on click, or a per-user setting stored with the other `userSettings`) and whether it persists; (3) saving: a preset saved while extra layers are on stores all of their sections, so it would replace everything even in additive mode; decide whether save lets you pick which layers a preset includes.

- Logo Zoom follow-ups (the Zoom checkbox, the logo pad and the suggested zoom point are in). The suggested point (`utils/zoomFocus.ts`, rule tuned on 56 real and 58 synthetic images): aim at the middle of the visible shape's bounding box, pulled toward the strongest radial-symmetry and blob-contrast peak by how confident that peak is. It is applied when a logo is picked and when an `@user` logo loads (a saved preset otherwise keeps its own focus); drag the dot to adjust it, double-click the dot to return to it. Known weak spots: a bright small detail on a flat shape can win when its peak is above about 0.5; rings with a tiny, fine center are found less reliably than wide-banded ones; faces are not looked for (eyes are not detected), so a portrait lands on the torso or the middle; the rule only clearly beats the plain center on sprites with a real focal point. Still open: (1) Feather: soften each nested copy's edge into the copy behind it so opaque images blend like a real zoom instead of showing a rectangle within a rectangle. (2) Whether the logo's tilt should follow its position as well as the camera, and whether the position range should let the logo leave the screen (`positionX/Y_MIN/MAX` in `logo.config.ts`).
