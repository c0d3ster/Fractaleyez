Effective date: [effective date]

This policy explains what data Fractaleyez (run by c0d3ster LLC, "we", "us") collects, why, and who handles it for us. It goes with our Terms of Service.

1. Your microphone and camera stay on your device

The visualizer listens to your microphone, and to your camera if you turn on the camera layer. Both are processed in your browser to draw the visuals. We never record, upload or store your audio or video. Your browser asks for permission first, and you can revoke it at any time in its settings.

2. What we collect

  • Account details, if you sign in: your account id, display name, email address and profile photo, provided by our sign-in provider Clerk (directly or from the social account you sign in with).
  • Settings and content you save: visualizer settings (such as crossfade time, quality and HUD options), saved presets, packs, and images you upload as particle or logo sprites. If your sign-in account has a profile photo, we make a copy of it as your logo sprite.
  • Plan and billing status: your plan, your Stripe customer and subscription ids, the subscription status, the renewal or end date, and whether it is set to cancel. We do not receive or store your card number.
  • Usage and device data: anonymous page views and basic device and browser information through Vercel Analytics and Google Analytics, and standard server logs (such as IP address and request times) kept by our hosting provider.

3. How we use it

  • To run the Service: sign you in, save and load your settings, presets, packs and images.
  • To bill paid plans and apply the plan you paid for. We give Stripe your email and display name so it can send receipts and fill in Checkout.
  • To understand how the Service is used and keep it fast and working.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping billing records.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for targeted advertising.

5. Uploaded images are public

Images you upload are served from a public URL so the visualizer can load them. Anyone with the URL can see the image. Do not upload anything you want to keep private.

6. Data stored in your browser

The app keeps some data in your browser's local storage so it loads quickly and works offline: cached preset lists and preset settings, your quality tier, your chosen camera, panel layout preferences and audio timing calibration. Clerk sets cookies to keep you signed in, and Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits. You can clear these in your browser settings.
